## What is real and what is synthetic
- The case is a **synthetic distribution center** generated by `fdekit demo` (800 orders, 1,800 SKUs). Rotulated in the video.
- Every figure comes from that demo's `A3.md` / `hallazgos.json`: 54.12% conformance (433/800), 31 paths, 157.2 m per order (median), 25.6% of SKUs with defects (95% CI 21.1-30.8, sample of 317), 61.2% of waiting in ORDER_RELEASED to PICK. Reproducible: `fdekit demo`.
- Spaghetti diagram and heatmap are real `fdekit` outputs.
- Book techniques are paraphrased with title and author, no quotes. The knowledge graph declares itself a synthesis from general knowledge (no book text ingested), so verify each technique name against the book before publishing.
- The 1951 coal-mine line comes from the manifesto (Trist and Bamforth, Human Relations); the paper's text was not read.
- No generated images or clips: everything is animated diagrams plus real kit charts.
