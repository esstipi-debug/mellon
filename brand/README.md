# Mellon Brand Assets & Visual Identity Guidelines 🗝️🌿

This directory contains the official brand assets, graphic files, and design specifications for **Mellon**.

---

## 1. Symbol Anatomy & Meaning

![Mellon Logo](mellon-logo.png)

The Mellon emblem is formed by three leaves converging into an **«M»**:
* **Left Leaf — Ground *(Terreno)*:** Wavy topographic contour lines representing physical terrain, loading docks, and material friction.
* **Center Leaf — Alliance *(Alianza)*:** Organic branching biological veins representing the human alliance that shelters, touches, and unites the other two.
* **Right Leaf — Rigor *(Rigor)*:** Parallel geometric lines at 45° representing measurement, audited precision, and the immutable ledger.

**Spatial Law:** The horizontal order is strictly immutable: **Ground (Left) · Alliance (Center) · Rigor (Right)**. The central leaf must always physically touch both adjacent leaves.

## 3. Usage Rules & Scaling

1. **Minimum Size:**
   * Full detail (with internal venation): Minimum display height of **64 px** (digital) or **18 mm** (print).
   * Below 64 px: Use the flat solid silhouette without internal vein lines to prevent line blurring.
2. **Clear Space:**
   * Maintain a minimum clear space surrounding the mark equal to the width of the central leaf ($1X$).
3. **Strict Prohibitions:**
   * Do not alter the spatial order of the leaves.
   * Do not rotate, skew, or distort the aspect ratio.
   * Do not replace the gold outline with arbitrary accent colors.

## 5. Provenance Note
*Mellon* and the Gate of Moria originate in J.R.R. Tolkien's literature (*«Speak, friend, and enter»*). They serve strictly as philosophical inspiration for entering enterprise operations as an ally rather than a conqueror. No copyrighted visual artwork or verbatim glyph from Tolkien's estate is reproduced.
