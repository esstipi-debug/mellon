## Job Breakdown — Important Steps, Key Points, Reasons

| # | Important step (what) | Key point (how — the knack that makes or breaks it) | Reason (why — **Q** = quality, **S** = safety, **F** = flow) | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | **Stop and confirm the location is truly empty** | Look at all levels of the bay and both adjacent slots, at eye level, using the headlamp. Do not reach blind into upper levels. | **Q:** product displaced one slot or one level is the most frequent cause and needs no search. **S:** blind reaching on level 3–4 is a falling-product risk. | 20 s |
| 2 | **Record the exception on the RF before searching** | Press *Not Found* on the RF screen. Do not skip the line and come back later. | **Q:** an unrecorded exception never reaches the master-data fix, so it repeats tomorrow. The log is the defect report. | 15 s |
| 3 | **Look up alternative locations in the system** | Use the inventory-lookup screen for the SKU. Do not rely on memory or ask across the aisle. | **Q:** night shift handles SKUs it does not know by sight; memory-based search creates wrong picks. **F:** lookup is faster than walking. | 25 s |
| 4 | **Pick from the real location and scan *that* location label** | Scan the label of the slot where the product **was**, then the product. Two scans, every time. | **Q:** this scan is the only data point that lets the location master be corrected. Without it the exception recurs. | 20 s |
| 5 | **Resume the route from the RF sequence** | Return to the next RF task. Do not reorder the remaining picks yourself. | **F:** manual reordering breaks route optimization and adds walking for the rest of the wave. | 10 s |
| — | **Escalate if the SKU is not found anywhere** | After step 3 shows no stock, press *Short* and notify the team lead on the radio. Do not search other zones. | **Q:** prevents a phantom-inventory short from being hidden as a delay. **S:** no unplanned travel in active forklift aisles. | — |

**Never:** climb racking; use a pallet as a step; pick from a location without scanning it; write the real location on paper "to enter later."

## Delivery — The 4 Steps of Job Instruction (for the Trainer)

### Step 1 — Prepare the Operator
- Put the operator at ease. State the purpose: *"This card exists because the system's location data is wrong sometimes. You are the one who fixes it with one scan."*
- Ask what they already know: *"Show me what you did the last time a location was empty."* (Past action, not opinion. See the Gemba protocol.)
- Get them interested: show the zone's exception count from last week's event log.
- Position them correctly: standing at the actual bay, RF in hand, not in the break room.

### Step 2 — Present the Operation
- **Pass 1:** perform the job and state each **important step** aloud.
- **Pass 2:** perform it again, stating each step **and** its **key point**.
- **Pass 3:** perform it a third time, stating step, key point, **and reason**.
- Present one point at a time. Do not give more than the operator can absorb on night shift (fatigue lowers absorption; keep each session under 10 minutes).

### Step 3 — Try-Out Performance
- The operator performs the job while the trainer watches silently. Correct errors immediately and calmly.
- The operator performs it again, stating each **important step** aloud.
- Again, stating step and **key point**.
- Again, stating step, key point, and **reason**.
- Continue until the trainer is certain the operator knows it. Minimum: 3 correct, unprompted repetitions.

### Step 4 — Follow Up
- Put them on their own and name **who to ask**: the night-shift team lead, then the on-call deployment engineer during the 4-week hypercare window.
- Check frequently: twice in the first shift, once per shift for the first week.
- Encourage questions; taper coaching as the operator stabilizes.
- **Verify with data, not impressions:** each week compare this operator's `PICK_EXCEPTION_LOCATION` events against correct real-location scans (step 4). A rate below 95% means retrain on step 4.

> *If the worker hasn't learned, the instructor hasn't taught.* (TWI JI principle.)
