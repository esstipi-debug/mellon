# Dossier: The Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto)
**Key:** `pp` | **Target Practice:** Forward Deployed Engineering & Deployment Strategy

---

## 1. Core Thesis in 5 Lines
1. Executive communication must lead with the answer first (top-down); leaders lack the time to trace your chronological analytical journey.
2. Every document or presentation must have a single Governing Thought supported by a logically grouped hierarchy of ideas.
3. The entry narrative must follow the SCQA formula (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer) to align with what the reader already knows.
4. Supporting ideas in any group must be MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) and ordered by time, structural part, or ranking.
5. In the vertical dimension, every statement must trigger a logical question in the reader’s mind (Why? How?) answered by the layer beneath it.

---

## 2. Chapter-by-Chapter Map

* **Ch 1: Why a Pyramid Structure?:** The psychology of reading. Human working memory holds $\sim 7 \pm 2$ items; unorganized details cause mental fatigue. Top-down ordering matches brain processing.
* **Ch 2: The Substructures within the Pyramid:** The vertical relationship (question/answer dialogue) and the horizontal relationship (deductive chains vs. inductive groupings).
* **Ch 3: How to Build a Pyramid Structure:** The top-down method (Start with SCQA $\rightarrow$ Governing Thought $\rightarrow$ Key Line) vs. the bottom-up method (List points $\rightarrow$ find common denominators $\rightarrow$ infer the governing insight).
* **Ch 4: Fine-Tuning the Deductive and Inductive Relationships:** Why inductive groupings are superior for executives; minimizing complex deductive syllogisms which are fragile.
* **Ch 5: The Introductory Flow (SCQA):** Setting the context: Situation (undisputed ground), Complication (the trigger/pain), Question (the dilemma), Answer (your recommendation).
* **Ch 6: Ordering Your Ideas (MECE):** The three logical orders: Time order (steps in a process), Structural order (parts of a system/geography), Degree order (ranking by significance).
* **Ch 7: Grouping and Summarizing:** The danger of "blanket headings" (e.g., "Financials"). Every heading must state an actionable thought or takeaway.
* **Ch 8: Putting It into Words:** Formatting techniques: Headings, bullet hierarchies, transitional phrases, and slide title discipline (action titles).

## 3. Step-by-Step Toolkits for a Deployed Strategist

### A. The SCQA Executive Memo Blueprint
Use for Pilot Evaluation Memos, Budget Requests, or Architecture Recommendations:
* **Situation (S):** State a common baseline fact that the client agrees with.
  * *"In Q1, Plant 4 committed to reducing equipment downtime by 20% to meet new customer volume targets."*
* **Complication (C):** State the roadblock or shift that makes the status quo untenable.
  * *"However, telemetry data shows that Line 2 has suffered 42 unplanned micro-stops this month due to sensor calibration drift, threatening the Q2 shipping schedule."*
* **Question (Q):** State the explicit dilemma on the executive's desk.
  * *"How should Plant 4 prevent sensor drift on Line 2 without shutting down operations for maintenance?"*
* **Answer (Governing Thought / BLUF):** State the recommendation immediately.
  * *"Deploy automated edge-filtering algorithms across Line 2 sensors next week, which will eliminate 85% of false alerts at an estimated implementation cost of \$18k."*

### B. The Inductive Key-Line Structure (MECE)
Directly beneath the Answer, provide 3 MECE supporting pillars:
1. **Pillar 1 (Technical Viability):** Proven effectiveness in sandbox testing.
2. **Pillar 2 (Financial Return):** Direct ROI and downtime savings of \$140k/quarter.
3. **Pillar 3 (Operational Simplicity):** Can be deployed by local plant technicians in under 4 hours.

## 5. Field Playbook for Deployed Strategists

| Trigger in Deployment | FDE Action | Artifact / Script |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive asks for a project status update.** | Do not recount daily activities; deliver Answer First. | *"Project is on schedule to deploy next Friday, with one open data pipeline risk we are resolving today."* |
| **Writing the Final Pilot Recommendation Report.** | Structure via SCQA intro followed by MECE Key Line. | Deliver a 1-page executive summary memo; move all code schemas and charts to Appendix. |
| **Slide deck presentation to Board / Steering Committee.** | Enforce Action Titles on every slide. | Instead of title *"Downtime Metrics"*, write: *"Line 2 Downtime Dropped 38% During Week 3 of the Pilot"*. |
| **Team slide deck is unfocused and wandering.** | Audit horizontal groupings for MECE compliance. | Ensure each bullet category follows Time, Structure, or Degree order. |
